# Participant Information Sheet (PIS) Checklist
*Essential components for mental health survey questionnaires*

1. **Academic Affiliation Header:**
   - Official name of the medical college / university / department.
   - Name and official institutional designation of Principal Investigator (PI) and Co-Investigators.

2. **Study Objective in Plain Language:**
   - Clear, jargon-free explanation of what research question is being evaluated.
   - Why this specific group (e.g. resident doctors, postpartum mothers, youth) was selected.

3. **Duration & Demands:**
   - Realistic completion time estimate (e.g., "Approximately 8 to 10 minutes").
   - Number of sections or questions.

4. **Voluntariness & Right to Withdraw:**
   - Explicit confirmation that declining to participate will have no impact on employment, grades, or medical care.
   - Unconditional exit rights.

5. **Data Protection & Anonymity:**
   - Clarify whether responses are collected without email/IP identifiers.
   - Where data will be archived (e.g. encrypted institutional drive).
   - Compliance with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India.

6. **Distress Protocol & Helpline References:**
   - Clear inclusion of 24/7 national helplines: **Tele-MANAS (14416 / 1800-891-4416)** and **KIRAN (1800-599-0019)**.

7. **Institutional Ethics Committee Contact:**
   - Member Secretary or office contact details of the reviewing Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC).
