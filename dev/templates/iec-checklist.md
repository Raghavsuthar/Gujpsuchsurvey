# Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) Submission Checklist
*Pre-submission self-audit for PIs and medical residents submitting to GujPsychSurvey*

- [ ] **Valid Ethics Committee Registration:**
  - IEC is registered with CDSCO or DHR (Department of Health Research, Govt of India).
  - Registration format conforms to `ECR/xxx/Inst/GJ/xxxx`.

- [ ] **Letter Details Verified:**
  - Clearance certificate is on official institutional letterhead.
  - Signed by the Member Secretary or Chairperson of the Ethics Committee.
  - Full title of the study matches the title entered in the protocol form verbatim.
  - Names of all listed co-investigators are present.
  - Validity expiry date is specified and in the future.

- [ ] **External Questionnaire Verification:**
  - URL starts with `https://`.
  - Survey is accessible publicly without requiring third-party organization domain logins (or explicitly specifies required institutional credentials).
  - First screen has informed consent and participant information sheet.
  - No payment or banking details requested.

- [ ] **Vernacular Language Equivalence:**
  - If Gujarati or Hindi forms are used, back-translation or bilingual validation certification is noted.
