What to Do Right Now: Gentle Steps for Acute Distress

If you are overwhelmed, pause and take small, grounded actions one moment at a time.

1

Pause & Breathe

Inhale slowly for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, and exhale for 6 seconds. Repeat 5 times.

2

Ground Your Senses

Name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, and 1 you can taste.

3

Connect with Someone

Call a trusted friend, family member, mentor, or dial the Tele-MANAS helpline at 14416.

4

Postpone Decisions

Give yourself permission to defer all major decisions until tomorrow morning or until after speaking to a doctor.

When to Seek Professional Consultation

Consider scheduling an appointment with a registered psychiatrist or clinical psychologist if you notice:

check_box Persistent sadness, emptiness, or tearfulness lasting over 2 weeks
check_box Severe insomnia, early morning awakenings, or unrefreshing sleep
check_box Sudden panic attacks, rapid heartbeat, or feeling loss of control
check_box Loss of interest in hobbies, occupational burnout, or social withdrawal
