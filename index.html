How GujPsychSurvey Works

A transparent, non-custodial bridge connecting investigator-hosted protocols with informed participants.

1

1. IEC-Approved Protocol Submission

Investigators and medical residents submit protocols with valid Institutional Ethics Committee clearance certificates.

2

2. Independent Moderator Review

Protocols undergo scrutiny for valid ethical approval, non-commercial intent, zero tracking, and informed consent.

Protocols are reviewed within 3 to 5 working days.
3

3. Public Discovery & Direct Linkage

Participants discover verified studies and link directly to investigator-hosted secure platforms (REDCap, Google Forms, Qualtrics). No response data is ever stored on this repository.
