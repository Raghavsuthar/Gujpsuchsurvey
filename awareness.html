Participating in Mental Health Research

Everything you need to know about your rights, protections, and voluntary role in building psychiatric knowledge in Gujarat and across India.

Why Participate in Academic Research?

Psychiatric epidemiology and cultural psychology rely on genuine community participation to design better healthcare interventions.

translate

Cultural & Linguistic Relevance

Most psychological instruments were created in Western nations. Your responses help validate vernacular Gujarati and Hindi questionnaires suited for local community idioms of distress.

local_hospital

Informing Public Health Policy

Research by postgraduate residents and medical faculties shapes rural clinic staffing, postpartum screening protocols, and crisis response infrastructure across Gujarat.

record_voice_over

Reducing Community Stigma

Answering questions about burnout, stress, sleep, or emotional health normalizes these discussions and breaks generational taboos around seeking psychiatric care.

Participant Protections & Critical Cautions

Never compromise your personal security or privacy. Keep these absolute safeguards in mind.

gpp_bad Red Flags: Beware of Malicious or Fake Surveys

Legitimate academic research NEVER asks for:

  • Bank account numbers, IFSC codes, credit card CVVs, or UPI PINs.
  • Aadhaar numbers or OTP SMS verification codes.
  • Registration or administrative participation fees.

If any external questionnaire asks for financial information, immediately close the tab and report it to the moderator.

verified Your Inalienable Rights
  • 100% Voluntariness: You have the absolute right to decline any question or refuse to begin.
  • Immediate Unconditional Withdrawal: You may close the questionnaire at any second. You do not need to give any explanation.
  • Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) Clearance: You have the right to see the ethics registration number on the information sheet.
  • Investigator Accountability: The PI's institutional email and affiliated college department must be clearly printed on the consent page.

health_and_safety What if a questionnaire causes you emotional distress?

Reflecting on mood or trauma can sometimes trigger distress. If you feel uncomfortable, stop answering immediately.
