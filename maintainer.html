Platform Mission & Moderation Policy

Modeled in the spirit of academic repositories like PsyToolkit, GujPsychSurvey serves the psychiatry, psychology, and public health community across Gujarat and India.

Many medical residents, assistant professors, and clinical psychologists conduct rigorous, high-value epidemiological surveys as part of postgraduate dissertations or institutional research. Historically, these surveys circulated inefficiently on informal WhatsApp groups or social media feeds, leaving prospective participants skeptical regarding legitimacy and data safety.

GujPsychSurvey provides an open, human-moderated, non-commercial index where only studies verified by legitimate Institutional Ethics Committees (IEC) are listed.

How Listings Are Verified:

  • Institutional Registration: The investigator's medical college or university affiliation is verified through institutional directories or official domain correspondence.
  • IEC Stamp Audit: The signed, numbered Institutional Ethics Committee certificate is inspected for valid validity dates and formal registration with regulatory authorities (CDSCO / DHR).
  • Zero Commercial Harvesting: Every questionnaire is manually tested to verify that it does not solicit bank accounts, passwords, or registration fees.
  • Bilingual Consent Check: Verification that participants are informed of their right to withdraw before submitting answers.

Academic & Clinical Disclaimer

Legal boundaries and regulatory scope of this directory.

1. No Clinical Diagnostic or Crisis Services: GujPsychSurvey is strictly an informational index. It does not provide medical consultations, psychiatric diagnoses, psychological therapy, or crisis intervention.

2. Third-Party Questionnaire Content: All surveys listed on this site are designed, hosted, and administered independently by their respective Principal Investigators on external platforms (e.g. REDCap, Google Forms, Qualtrics). GujPsychSurvey assumes no responsibility for third-party survey logic, data collection failures, or investigator communication.

3. Participation is Entirely Voluntary: Any individual choosing to participate in a research survey does so voluntarily. Participants retain the complete right to exit at any time without penalty.

4. Right to Withdraw or Remove Listings: The honorary moderator panel reserves the right to unlist or archive any survey record immediately if protocol violations, ethics revocations, or complaints are verified.

Privacy & Data Governance Policy

Formulated under the principles of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India.

What GujPsychSurvey Stores:

  • Zero Server-Side Storage: There is no backend database. This website is statically hosted on GitHub Pages. No user survey responses or IP access logs are harvested by this repository.
  • Zero Tracking or Advertising Cookies: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or commercial fingerprinting scripts.
  • Local Browser Storage (localStorage): The site uses small client-side keys exclusively in your local browser to remember:
    • Your preferred interface language (gujpsych_lang: en, gu, or hi)
    • Your in-progress submission draft (gujpsych_submission_draft) saved strictly inside your browser for recovery.
  • Public Investigator Contact: Principal Investigator contact emails and phone numbers submitted during moderation are kept confidential and are not published publicly unless explicitly configured in config.json.
