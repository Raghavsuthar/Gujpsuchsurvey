Protocol Moderation & Lifecycle Process

A standardized, 5-stage pathway ensuring zero commercial exploitation and total compliance with ICMR National Ethical Guidelines (2017).

1

Prepare IEC Clearance

Obtain full, unconditioned Institutional Ethics Committee approval from your medical college or university. Ensure the clearance letter contains an official registration number and valid dates.

2

Build External Form with Consent Sheet

Configure your questionnaire on an external platform (Google Forms, REDCap, Qualtrics, KoboToolbox). The first screen must feature the complete Participant Information Sheet and voluntary Informed Consent in English and vernacular Gujarati/Hindi.

3

Submit for Moderation

Fill the submission form below. Provide study synopsis, investigator affiliations, ethics approval numbers, and a shareable drive link to your clearance letter.

4

Moderator Review

Honorary moderators audit affiliation authenticity, valid ethics stamp, absence of commercial data harvesting, and questionnaire usability within 3 to 5 business days.

5

Published & Subsequent Archival

Your study goes live in the public directory with an official reference code. When data collection terminates or ethics expiry passes, the record seamlessly transitions to the permanent archive.
